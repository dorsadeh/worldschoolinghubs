# Candidate-hub assessment — validity, seasonality, nationality (2026-06-09)

Companion to **`candidate-hubs-2026-06-08.csv`** (97 candidates). For each candidate, three things:
1. **Valid/Relevant confidence** — is it real, still operating, and a genuine *family* hub?
2. **Seasonality** — the family window (normalized; "Year-round (term)" for standing schools).
3. **Dominant nationality** — only where a real skew exists; else "Mixed intl".

**Note vs. the CSV's `confidence` column:** that one rated *trust in the captured data*; this rates
*is-it-valid-and-still-relevant* — a different axis. Everything is still UNVALIDATED unless `basis`
shows **✓** (I live-checked it this pass). Otherwise inferred from source-count + the 3 research
passes (ChatGPT/Gemini/mine) + nationality research.

**Confidence rubric:** **High** = confirmed active / multi-directory + clear site + specifics ·
**Med** = single listing, plausible, unverified · **Low** = vague/"verify", or an online/FB/directory
(discovery channel, not a hub) · **Upcoming** = not open yet · **DUP** = likely duplicate (don't double-count).
