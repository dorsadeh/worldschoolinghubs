# Worldschooling / family hubs — master taxonomy by TYPE (2026-06-09)

A consolidated, **packaged** view of everything from all research passes, sorted by the *kind*
of hub rather than by place. This is the map-ready typology; the evidence, sources, seasons,
counts, and caveats live in **`family-gathering-places-2026-06-08.md`** (don't duplicate counts
here — follow the links). All of it is still **UNVALIDATED** blog/press-derived signal.

## The five categories (and where the lines are)

| # | Type | Definition | Who runs it | Examples |
|---|------|------------|-------------|----------|
| 1 | **Organic hubs** | A *destination* where families self-cluster for a season. No central operator — the town *is* the hub. | Nobody / the community | Pai, Bansko-summer, Goa, Chiang Mai, Koh Lanta |
| 2 | **Permanent / commercial hubs** | A *standing place or operator* you can join on an **ongoing** basis (available most of the year). Two sub-types: **2a commercial** (paid campuses) and **2b intentional communities / eco-villages**. | A company or a resident community | Boundless Life, Green School Bali, The Hive; Sentira, Spirala, Progetto Baita |
| 3 | **Pop-up hubs** | An **ephemeral** gathering — **different organizers and/or different attendees each edition**; not a continuous operation. Appears, then is gone. | Whoever hosts that edition | World School Pop-Up Hub editions, local seasonal meetups, KL week-long pop-ups |
| 4 | **Traveling programs** | **One operation that changes location** with **consistent guides/staff** (the cohort often moves together). Same people, new place. | A single operator, touring | THINK Global School, Together Explorers, Project World School, Deliberate Detour |
| 5 | **Spanish-immersion clusters** | A family cluster organized **around cheap Spanish schools + homestays** — the *school* is the social hub. Education-first, low-cost. | Local language schools | Antigua, Lake Atitlán, Oaxaca, San Cristóbal, Sucre |

### Cross-cutting attribute (applies to ALL five): Participation — **drop-off vs. whole-family**

This is a *tag*, not a category — every hub above is also one of:
- **Drop-off** 🎒 — kids are enrolled in a school/program; parents are elsewhere (working/living
  nearby). The hub is really a *school the family orbits*. → THINK Global School, Green School,
  Koh Lanta Swedish schools, HAIS Hoi An, Narnia/Arkee, Sas Academy, kids-only Spanish classes.
- **Whole-family** 👪 — the family participates *together* (co-living, community, or traveling as a
  unit). → all Organic hubs by nature, Boundless co-living, Together Explorers, Deliberate Detour,
  family Spanish programs, eco-villages.
- **Mixed** 🔀 — the operator offers both (Project World School: teen retreats lean drop-off; its
  Family Summit is whole-family).

**Key boundary calls:**
- **Pop-up vs. Traveling** = is it **the same operation moving** (traveling — consistent guides/
  staff) or a **fresh edition each time** (pop-up — new organizers/attendees)? ⚠️ **World School
  Pop-Up Hub** is named "pop-up" but if *one team* runs all 17 cities it's really **traveling** —
  confirm the staffing before final placement.
- **Permanent vs. Pop-up/Traveling** = does the place/operator persist year-round (permanent), or
  exist only on dates (pop-up/traveling)?
- **Boundless Life** → **2a (permanent/commercial)** because campuses + operator persist, **but**
  it's cohort-delivered, so it straddles traveling. Re-slot if you prefer rhythm over place.
- **Bansko-winter** = a **fixed-location seasonal recurring program** (same operator, doesn't move)
  — neither traveling (no location change) nor classic pop-up (not a fresh team each time). Hybrid.
- **Wonder Year is NOT a program** — it's a how-to **book/resource** (you cited it as an example,
  but it doesn't operate hubs); kept in references, not as a traveling hub.
- **Towns can hold several types at once** — see the overlap matrix at the bottom. A feature, not a
  contradiction (a family can pick its mode in one place).

## 4 — Traveling programs (one operation + consistent staff, changing location)

The cohort/operator moves together; **at least the guides & staff are consistent** across stops
(this is what separates it from a pop-up). Often the *families themselves* travel as a group.

## 5 — Spanish-immersion clusters (school = the social hub)

Cheap (~$5–10/hr) one-on-one + homestay + cultural activities; families stay weeks-to-months and
bond *through the school*. Best LatAm-highland window **Nov–Apr**; CR/Ecuador ~year-round.

**Tier 1 — famous school scene AND documented worldschooling-family clustering:**
- **Antigua** (Guatemala) — the classic since the '80s (Antigüeña, Ixchel, Mundo)
- **Lake Atitlán / San Pedro La Laguna** (Guatemala) — cooperative schools w/ kids & family courses
- **Oaxaca** (Mexico) — Instituto Cultural Oaxaca, Lingoax, Quihubole
- **San Cristóbal de las Casas** (Mexico) — Tierras Mayas, Fluenz

**Tier 2 — strong family-school infrastructure; clustering emerging / more expat:**
- **Sucre** (Bolivia) — cheapest; long-stay learner community
- **Costa Rica** (Heredia, Sámara, Tamarindo, Monteverde) — CPI & Intercultura whole-family immersion
- **Cuenca** (Ecuador) — colonial highland expat-family base
- **Granada** (Spain) — Escuela Montalbán / Enforex family courses (the EU option)
- **Medellín** (Colombia) — family programs but nomad-skewed city

2. **Pop-up vs. Traveling** — the discriminator is *consistent staff that moves*. Need to confirm
   staffing for borderline cases: **World School Pop-Up Hub** (one team across 17 cities → traveling?
